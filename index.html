"Time you enjoy wasting is not wasted time." — Marthe Troly-Curtin



Have you ever imagined your favorite musicians gathering all together ?

I deeply value the rich variety of nationalities represented by these musicians, as well as the distinctive, soulful qualities of their indie-style songs. Each artist brings their own cultural background and emotional depth, creating a sound that feels both intimate and unique.
It’s truly mind-blowing to imagine these voices — each shaped by different languages, landscapes, and experiences — resonating together in one space.

This website is a personal celebration of that blend: a quiet corner where international artistry meets heartfelt expression.
